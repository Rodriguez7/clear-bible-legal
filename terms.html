Clear Bible Plus

Monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions have no free trial. The annual plan is billed upfront for one year. The store shows the local price and billing period before confirmation. Manage cancellation and billing through Apple subscription settings; restore purchases from Plus.

Deleting your Clear Bible account or uninstalling the app does not cancel store billing. Contact support if purchase verification or restoration is delayed.
