Clear Bible Plus

Plus provides additional AI explanations, contextual questions and Study/Deep modes. Monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions are offered without a free trial. The annual plan is billed upfront for one year; it is not a monthly payment plan with a one-year commitment.

The store shows the local price and billing period before you confirm. Payment is charged to your store account at purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal through store subscription settings. Restore purchases is available in Plus. Apple's store rules and applicable consumer rights govern cancellation and refunds. Account deletion does not cancel a subscription.

Availability

Bundled and cached chapters can be read offline. New edition downloads, cloud synchronization and AI require a connection. AI requests can be unavailable during provider outages; this does not prevent free reading. Access after a purchase depends on store and server verification; Check access is available if verification is delayed.
