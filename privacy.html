Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

This notice covers the Clear Bible iOS app, its TestFlight versions and this support website. Available controls can vary by app version; the distinctions below explain the current beta and the updated app.

Clear Bible is provided by Rise (Kayembe Mukala), Tokiostraße 12/2/117, 1220 Vienna, Austria. For privacy requests, email support@syncher.online. Our business details appear in the imprint.

Reading without an account

You can read and save passages without signing in. Preferences, reading position, local notes, downloaded chapters and guest progress are stored on your device. Downloads contact GetBible, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Bundled and cached chapters work offline; new downloads require a connection.

Sign-in and account information

Google or Apple provides sign-in through Clerk. Clerk processes your account identifiers, name, email (including an Apple relay address) and any profile image supplied by the provider. Clear Bible does not request your provider password, phone contacts, GPS location, camera or photo library.

Clerk maintains authentication and security records, including device identifiers, browser/device information and IP addresses. Session activity may include an approximate city and country inferred from the IP address. Clerk client SDK telemetry is disabled in the current app; authentication/security records are separate. We do not use them for advertising.

Cloud library and progress

Cloud synchronization is optional. Select Sync library in Profile to send your library to Convex. The updated app also asks for separate cloud-storage permission before uploading personal library content; earlier TestFlight versions use the explicit Sync library action without that separate permission screen. A successful explicit sync enables subsequent foreground/network retries. Synced records include your account ID, optional private display name, reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, saved explanations, completed reading/lesson events, daily goals and reminder preferences. Signing in alone does not enable library background synchronization.

Notes and questions can include sensitive information, such as religious beliefs. We process the content you choose to use in these optional features to provide them. We do not assign a faith score or infer a religious identity for advertising. Avoid including unnecessary information about yourself or other people.

Optional AI explanations and questions

Before AI use, the app asks you to allow sharing. Our server sends the selected Scripture, your question and recent conversation to OpenRouter and Microsoft Azure, hosting GPT-4.1 mini. Your private library notes are excluded from prompts. AI interpretation is displayed separately from Scripture and can be mistaken.

The production route requires Azure-only, zero-data-retention-classified inference endpoints, denies data-collecting endpoints and has no fallback to other hosting providers. This endpoint restriction does not mean that all app records, usage metadata, security logs or backups are erased. Temporary in-memory prompt caching may be used under the endpoint classification.

Our server may cache validated AI answers under your account. The updated app adds a separate cloud-storage permission for saving these answers; the earlier TestFlight backend does not yet enforce that additional permission. If you choose to report an answer, the displayed answer and passage reference are saved for review; an answer can echo your question. Turn off AI sharing in Profile to stop future requests. Offline revocation is saved locally and retried when online. Revoking AI sharing does not by itself delete saved explanations or previous records.

Subscriptions and billing records

Apple handles iOS store payments; Clear Bible does not receive full payment-card details. RevenueCat and Convex use account IDs, transaction/purchase history, renewal and expiry status to verify Plus. RevenueCat also uses purchase records for subscription dashboard analytics. This billing analytics is separate from the optional app analytics setting.

Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple settings and restore purchases from Plus. Deleting an app account or uninstalling the app does not cancel store billing.

Reminders and optional app analytics

Reminders are scheduled locally after you choose a reminder and grant notification permission. You can change them in Profile or device settings.

Optional PostHog app analytics is not configured in the current distributed build and sends no events. The app contains an opt-in analytics control, separate from authentication records and RevenueCat subscription analytics. We will update this notice before changing the described collection. Choosing reminders does not enable app analytics.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives ordinary web connection information. The site has no app analytics scripts, advertisements or account forms. Support receives the messages and contact details you choose to send by email. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, private notes or payment-card information.

Retention and account deletion

  • Local saves and cached chapters remain on your device until removed by the app, you or the operating system. Signing out preserves account-specific local saves for a later sign-in.
  • Account-linked cloud records remain while the account is active unless content is removed or the account is deleted. Cache validity is not a guarantee of physical erasure when an answer expires.
  • The deletion worker removes the Clerk account and app-owned cloud records. It retains a pseudonymous deletion/security marker to prevent stale sessions or late billing events from recreating deleted data. That marker currently has no automatic expiry.
  • Stores, billing and hosting providers may retain separate transaction, security, operational or backup records under their own obligations. Account deletion is not a promise that every third-party record disappears immediately.

In the app, choose Profile → Delete account and confirm the request. The app shows its status and allows retry if needed. Legacy Apple sign-ins without retrievable tokens may also need manual removal in Apple Account settings. Contact support if you cannot access the app or need help with access, correction, export or deletion.

Service providers and transfers

Our service providers include Clerk (authentication), Convex (cloud storage/server functions), OpenRouter and Microsoft Azure (optional AI), RevenueCat and Apple (subscriptions), GetBible (edition downloads) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Services may process information outside your country, including in the United States. The providers publish data-processing terms that describe their safeguards, subprocessors and transfer mechanisms, including standard contractual clauses where applicable: Clerk, Convex, OpenRouter and RevenueCat. These terms do not mean that every provider retains every type of data for the same period, or that data stays exclusively in the European Union. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

Your choices and rights

Reading is available without an account. You can manage AI sharing, local reminders and library items in the app, and delete an account from Profile. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, export, erasure, restriction or objection, and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not undo prior processing. Contact support; ownership verification may be required.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the Austrian Datenschutzbehörde.

Why we use information

We use account information and requested cloud saves to provide the services you ask for; optional AI sharing and additional cloud-storage permission are choices you can withdraw. We use limited authentication, fraud-prevention and deletion-status records to protect accounts and operate the service, and retain records where required by applicable law. Reading does not require agreement to optional AI, cloud storage or analytics.

Notes and AI questions may reveal religious beliefs or other sensitive information. Only include information you intend to share for the selected feature. Use AI for questions about the text, and avoid including personal religious affiliations, health information, passwords or information about other people. Library notes are not automatically included in AI prompts. Permission to use AI is separate from permission to upload the library.

Age and account access

Accounts, cloud synchronization and AI are intended for people aged 16 and older. Guest reading remains available without an account. The updated app asks for an age declaration and, in applicable Apple regions, an Apple system age range before account access. Declining or an unconfirmed range keeps guest reading available. Earlier TestFlight versions do not include these new checks.

The updated app retains the declaration version, time and account-bound permission state. Apple age-range bounds are used temporarily; the app does not store a date of birth, identity document or the returned bounds. No custom guardian-approval flow is offered. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact support.

Privacy controls in the updated app

The updated app offers separate cloud-storage permission for personal notes, saved explanations and reading/learning progress. Declining keeps reading and saves on the device. Withdrawing pauses new uploads and queues the server withdrawal if offline; it does not erase earlier cloud records. AI questions are not stored as a durable chat-history feature. Some of these protections require both the updated app and its matching server update; they are not assurances about earlier TestFlight versions.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the described practices change, and ask again in the app where a new permission is needed. The effective date appears above. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@syncher.online.
