Support

Email support@syncher.online. Please include Clear Bible, your app version, device and a description of the issue. Never send your password, sign-in codes, payment-card information or private notes.

Sign in and profile

Continue with Google or Apple creates an account on your first visit and signs you into the same account later. Use the same provider/account to find your saved library. In Profile, choose Edit profile to set your private display name.

Restore Plus

Open Plus and choose Restore purchases using the store account that originally purchased the subscription. If payment succeeded but access is still pending, use Check access before attempting another purchase. Contact support if verification does not finish.

Cancel a subscription

On iPhone, open Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Clear Bible. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can also choose Manage subscription in Clear Bible. Uninstalling or deleting your Clear Bible account does not cancel store billing.

Delete your account

In Clear Bible, open Profile → Delete account. Read the consequences and confirm the permanent deletion. The app displays the request status and supports retrying a failed request. When deletion completes, the app removes its account data and signs you out.

Older Apple sign-ins may require manual removal of the connection in your Apple Account settings; the deletion screen tells you when that applies. Cancel your subscription separately in the store.

If you cannot access the app, email support with your request. We will need to verify account ownership before deleting data. Do not include authentication credentials.

Offline reading

Bundled and previously cached chapters can be read offline. A chapter that has not been downloaded needs an internet connection. AI explanations and cloud sync need a connection.
